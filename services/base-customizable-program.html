Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this program best for?

This program is ideal for experienced athletes who understand training principles, are self-motivated, and want a personalized program without the daily coaching support. It's perfect for athletes who value independence while still wanting expert programming.

How do I access my program?

After your consultation, you'll receive immediate access to TeamBuildr where your personalized program will be uploaded. You can track your workouts, message your coach, and view all program updates in one place.

What happens when the program is complete?

We'll review your results, discuss any adjustments needed, and create a new program for your next phase. Programs typically run 8-12 weeks and can be extended if needed.

Can I adjust my schedule?

Yes! This program is designed for independence. You can adjust the timing of your workouts based on your life circumstances. The program structure remains the same, but you control the schedule.

Is this more expensive than Premium Coaching?

At $250/month, the Base Customizable Program is significantly more affordable than Premium Coaching ($1,200/year). You get expert programming and regular updates at a fraction of the cost, perfect for athletes who want structure without daily coaching.

What if I don't know how to train?

This program is best for athletes with some training experience. If you're new to training, we'd recommend starting with the Daily Training Program ($75/month) which provides simple, effective workouts for any fitness level.

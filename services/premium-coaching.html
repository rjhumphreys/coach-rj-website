Frequently Asked Questions

How often are there check-ins?

Weekly! Every 15-minute video call is designed to keep you on track, answer your questions, and make any necessary adjustments to your program.

Do in-person coaching sessions include Premium Coaching?

Yes, in-person coaching sessions are available in addition to Premium Coaching. This can be a great option if you're training in person and want to maximize your coaching investment.

Can I pause training due to travel or injury?

Yes, we can pause your program at any time. When you're ready to continue, we'll review your progress and adjust accordingly. This flexibility is part of why I coach athletes with all kinds of schedules.

What if my schedule changes during the cycle?

Weekly check-ins give us the opportunity to adjust your program mid-cycle to fit your new schedule. We're designed to be flexible and adapt to your life.

How do you adjust the program mid-cycle?

Based on your progress, recovery status, and any injuries or life changes, we adjust volume, intensity, or exercises as needed. This is built into the coaching process to ensure you continue progressing safely and effectively.

What happens at the end of the program?

We review your progress together, assess what went well, and create a plan for your next phase—whether that's continuing with Premium Coaching, transitioning to Base Coaching, or taking a deload week to recover.

Premium Coaching

Individualized coaching for goal-driven athletes and people with a competition season, race, or trip ahead.

Overview

Premium Coaching is the most hands-on option. We start with a consultation and needs analysis, build an annual plan around your target event, and refine your training week by week.

What's Included

  • Full TeamBuildr access
  • Initial consultation, needs analysis and annual planning
  • Personalized training program
  • Individual exercise selection with progression and regression
  • Lifting technique coaching
  • Scheduled weekly check-ins for accountability, technique, lifestyle changes and progress monitoring
  • Priced at $375 a month

Who It's For

  • Athletes preparing for a specific competition season, race or trip
  • People who want a clear, individualized plan
  • Anyone who values regular check-ins and close coaching feedback

Outcomes and Benefits

  • A plan built around your goal and calendar
  • Better lifting technique and confidence
  • Consistent accountability and measurable progress

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between pre-built and customizable programs?

Pre-built programs are designed for specific goals or sports and can be used as-is. Customizable programs are personalized for your specific goals, current fitness level, and preferences. Pre-built is faster and more affordable, while customizable offers more personalization.

Can I modify pre-built programs?

Yes, you can adjust pre-built programs to better match your needs. However, you'll get more value from a customized program if you want training specifically tailored to your goals and current capabilities.

Which pre-built program should I choose?

During your consultation, we'll discuss your goals, current fitness level, and preferences to recommend the best pre-built program for you. Some programs may need minor adjustments, while others are nearly ready to go.

I've tried pre-built programs before - what's different here?

These programs are designed and refined by me based on years of coaching experience. They incorporate the latest training science and have been tested with a wide range of athletes. Plus, you get regular updates and ongoing support throughout the program.

Is this more expensive than Base Customizable Program?

Yes, at $150/month, Pre-Built Programs are more expensive than the Base Customizable Program ($250/month). However, pre-built programs are for athletes who want proven programs without the need for customization. If you want a fully personalized program, the Base Customizable Program may be more appropriate.

The Philosophy of Daily Training

Progress in training comes from consistency, not intensity. The Daily Training Program is built on the principle that showing up regularly, even if modestly, leads to steady improvement over time.

Consistency is King

Training 5 days a week intensely may not produce lasting results. Training daily, even at a modest level, builds the habit and leads to consistent progress.

Progressive Overload

Even small increases in volume, intensity, or difficulty compound over time. Daily training ensures you're consistently pushing slightly beyond your comfort zone.

Recovery Built-In

The program includes rest and recovery days. You train smart, not hard, allowing your body to adapt and improve.

Accessible for Everyone

No special equipment, no fancy techniques, no prior knowledge required. Just show up, do the work, and trust the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

I've never trained before - is this program for me?

Yes! This program is specifically designed for beginners. You'll start with simple, effective exercises and gradually increase as you improve. Don't be intimidated - the program is built for anyone.

How much time should I commit?

Each workout typically takes 30-60 minutes. With 5-10 workouts per week, you're looking at 2-5 hours per week. This is a sustainable commitment that many athletes maintain for years.

Do I need any special equipment?

No! The program uses minimal equipment - mainly dumbbells, a bench, and possibly a barbell. If you don't have this equipment, we'll use alternatives. You don't need to buy anything special to start.

How long will the program last?

This is a monthly subscription with continuous support. You'll receive new workouts each week, and we'll adjust the program as you progress. There's no "end" - just ongoing progression.

What if I miss a day?

Miss a day? No problem. Just get back on track the next day. Consistency over perfection is the goal. Showing up regularly, even with occasional absences, leads to progress.

I've heard about the Base Customizable Program - what's the difference?

The Daily Training Program is for anyone who wants simple, consistent training without needing to know what to do or customize a program. The Base Customizable Program ($250/month) is for experienced athletes who want a fully personalized program without daily coaching. Choose based on your experience level and training needs.

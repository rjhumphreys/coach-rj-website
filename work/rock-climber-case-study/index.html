Rock Climber Case Study

Exercise physiology case study examining the demands of competitive climbing and how to train for them.

April 2023

Climbing is a strength-endurance sport with a big technical layer on top. Programming for it the way you'd program for a powerlifter or a ball-sport athlete misses what actually limits performance — usually some combination of grip endurance, body tension, and the ability to recover between attempts.

This exercise physiology case study profiles a competitive climber, breaks down the metabolic and structural demands of the sport, and outlines a training approach that targets those demands without sacrificing climbing-specific skill development.

Coach RJ holds an undergraduate degree in exercise science and is currently pursuing his master's in strength and conditioning. Before that, he spent four years at Stratton Mountain School in southern Vermont, working with winter sports athletes — coaching, programming, and developing the kind of interpersonal skills that turn a good program into one athletes actually buy into.

Today he coaches college athletes at Colby-Sawyer College. His experience spans training facility work, weight room supervision, and international offseason training programs.

![Coach RJ](/assets/images/rj-feature.jpg)

## Coaching philosophy

The foundation is passion and energy — fostering an environment that values learning and growth over results and ego. Athletes are partners in the work, not subjects of it. That means autonomy and education sit at the center of how every program is built.

<div class="values">
  <div class="value">
    <h4>Consistency over intensity</h4>
    <p>The work that compounds is the work that gets done — not the heroic week followed by three quiet ones.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="value">
    <h4>Enjoyment over ego</h4>
    <p>Athletes who like the process stick with the process. Ego is fragile; enjoyment is durable.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="value">
    <h4>Process over outcome</h4>
    <p>Outcomes are downstream of habits. Get the inputs right and the outputs follow.</p>
  </div>
</div>

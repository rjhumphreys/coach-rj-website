Premium Coaching

Individualized Coaching, Start to Finish

A clear, personalized plan for athletes working toward a competition season, race, or trip.

Premium Coaching is built for people with a specific goal and a date to hit it. We begin with a consultation and needs analysis, map out your year, then build and adjust your training week by week on the TeamBuildr platform.

What's Included

  • Full TeamBuildr access: logging, PR tracking, demo videos, media and journal uploads, and two-way coach/athlete communication
  • Initial consultation, needs analysis and annual planning
  • Personalized training program
  • Individual exercise selection with progression and regression
  • Lifting technique coaching
  • Scheduled weekly check-ins for accountability, technique, lifestyle changes and progress monitoring

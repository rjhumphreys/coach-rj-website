About Coach RJ

Strength coach, lifelong learner, and believer in process over ego.

Coach RJ holds a graduate degree in Exercise Science with a concentration in Strength and Conditioning from Colby-Sawyer College, where he also worked as an assistant strength and conditioning coach. He is passionate about building resilient, courageous athletes through a process-driven approach.

Before graduate school, Coach RJ worked at Stratton Mountain School, a college-preparatory high school specializing in winter sports. There, he coached alpine ski racing and worked as a strength and conditioning coach in the High Performance Department. He traveled across North America, South America, and Europe, coaching ski racers on and off the hill and helping to build a successful year-round program.

Today, Coach RJ draws on nearly a decade of coaching, education, and personal training experience to work with individuals and teams in winter, team, mountain, and outdoor sports.

Coaching Philosophy

The foundation is passion and energy—fostering an environment that values learning and growth over results and ego. Athletes are partners in the work, not subjects of it.

Consistency over intensity

The work that compounds is the work that gets done — not the heroic week followed by three quiet ones.

Enjoyment over ego

Athletes who enjoy the process stick with the process. Ego is fragile; enjoyment is durable.

Process over outcome

Outcomes are downstream of habits. Get the inputs right and the outputs follow.
